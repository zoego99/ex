3 Excel 建議公式（輔助欄位貼上使用）
Excel formula · 儲存格 A1 為金額 
=IF(A1>=100000000,
  TEXT(INT(A1/100000000),"#,##0")&"億"
  &IF(MOD(INT(A1/10000),10000)>0,TEXT(MOD(INT(A1/10000),10000),"#,##0")&"萬","")
  &IF(MOD(A1,10000)>0,TEXT(MOD(A1,10000),"#,##0"),"")&"元",
  IF(A1>=10000,
    TEXT(INT(A1/10000),"#,##0")&"萬"
    &IF(MOD(A1,10000)>0,TEXT(MOD(A1,10000),"#,##0"),"")&"元",
    TEXT(A1,"#,##0")&"元"
  )
)
